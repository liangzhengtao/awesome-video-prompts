## Food Content

### Satisfying Ramen Assembly
**Prompt:** Close-up top-down shot of a ramen bowl being assembled. First: broth is ladled in — rich, golden tonkotsu, steam rising. Then: noodles are placed with chopsticks, folded perfectly. Chashu pork is laid on one side. A soft-boiled egg is halved — the yolk is perfectly jammy, oozing slightly. Green onions are scattered. Nori is placed upright. Final: chili oil drizzled in a spiral. Each ingredient lands in slow-motion with a satisfying "plop" sound effect. The bowl rotates 360 degrees on a turntable. ASMR aesthetic, warm overhead lighting, shallow depth of field.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Satisfying food assembly that makes viewers hungry. Each layer adds color and texture. The turntable rotation shows every angle. TikTok food content gold.

**Variations:**
- Burger build: patty, cheese melting, lettuce, sauce drizzle, top bun slam
- Açaí bowl: purple base, granola sprinkle, fruit arrangement, honey drizzle
- Pizza: dough stretch, sauce spread, cheese pull, topping placement

### Cheese Pull Close-Up
**Prompt:** A slice of pizza is lifted from the box. The cheese stretches in a perfect, continuous pull — golden, bubbling, stretching 18 inches before separating. The camera is side-on, macro, catching every strand of mozzarella. Oil glistens on the surface. A basil leaf is visible, perfectly placed. The cheese finally breaks — snaps back onto the pizza with a satisfying bounce. Steam rises from the break point. The pizza is set down. The camera pushes into the melted cheese surface: microscopic bubbles, oil pools, char marks on the crust.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** The quintessential cheese pull shot. Stretchy, golden, satisfying. The macro push-in at the end adds quality. Viral food content.

**Variations:**
- Grilled cheese: cross-section cut, cheese oozing out both sides
- Fondue: bread dipping, cheese dripping, long strings
- Korean corn dog: mozzarella pull, golden batter, sugar crystals visible

### Chocolate Lava Cake
**Prompt:** A chocolate lava cake sits on a white plate. A spoon breaks through the outer shell — the interior erupts with molten dark chocolate. The lava flows slowly, dramatically, across the plate. The camera is at plate level, catching the flow in profile. Powdered sugar falls from above like snow. A single raspberry rolls into the chocolate river. The spoon scoops a piece — cake, lava, raspberry — and lifts it toward camera. The chocolate drips. ASMR: the crack of the shell, the flow of the lava, the clink of the spoon on porcelain. Dark background, single warm spotlight.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Dramatic dessert moment — the lava eruption is the hero. The slow flow across the plate creates a cinematic food moment. Dark, moody, luxurious.

**Variations:**
- Crème brûlée: torch flame, caramelizing sugar, cracking through with a spoon
- Soufflé: rising in the oven, impossibly tall, jiggling when removed
- Matcha dessert: green tea white chocolate, different color story

## Fashion & Transitions

### Outfit Change Snap
**Prompt:** A person stands in the center of frame, casual pose. They snap their fingers — instant outfit change. First: oversized hoodie and sneakers (streetwear). Snap: now a tailored blazer and turtleneck (smart casual). Snap: now a full evening gown/tuxedo (formal). Snap: back to the hoodie, smiling. Each transition is a single frame jump cut — no transition effect, just instant. The background stays the same (plain white studio). The lighting adjusts with each outfit: cool for streetwear, warm for smart casual, dramatic for formal. Camera: static, full body, centered.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Satisfying outfit transformation that shows range. The instant cuts are the hook. Each outfit gets exactly one second. Fashion TikTok staple.

**Variations:**
- Seasonal: summer outfit → autumn → winter → spring, same person
- Decades: 1960s → 1980s → 2000s → 2020s, different fashion eras
- Work to weekend: office attire → gym clothes → going-out outfit

### Fabric in Slow Motion
**Prompt:** A model walks toward the camera in a flowing silk dress. They spin at the halfway point. The dress fans out in extreme slow motion — every fold, every ripple, every wave of fabric is visible. Light passes through the silk, creating a luminous effect. The fabric settles back down as the model stops. The camera captures the fabric from multiple angles during the spin: side, below, from above. The texture of the silk is visible at macro level — individual fibers catching light. Single-color background (deep navy) to let the fabric be the star.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Fashion editorial meets slow-motion cinematography. The fabric movement IS the content. Satisfying, shareable, aspirational. Works for any flowing garment.

**Variations:**
- Cape blowing in wind: hero shot, dramatic, backlit
- Tulle skirt: multiple layers, ballerina spin, pink or white
- Denim jacket: thrown over shoulder, heavy fabric, different weight

### Mirror Transition
**Prompt:** A person walks past a full-length mirror. Their reflection doesn't match — the reflection is wearing something different and keeps walking past while the real person stops. The real person steps back to the mirror. This time, they're perfectly synced. They touch the mirror — their hand goes THROUGH the glass. They step through the mirror into a different room (different color palette, different lighting). Camera follows them through. On the other side: a different world. They look back — the mirror shows their original room, shrinking as they walk away.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** A clean mirror transition trick that's popular on social media. The moment the hand goes through the glass is the hook. Simple setup, impressive result.

**Variations:**
- Phone screen: person scrolls, the phone content becomes the real world
- Door: opening a door reveals a completely different location
- Water puddle: stepping into a puddle transports to a different place

## Comedy & Viral

### Expectation vs. Reality Split
**Prompt:** Split screen. Left side (Expectation): A person gracefully applies makeup in a well-lit bathroom. Perfect winged eyeliner, flawless skin, glamorous result. Right side (Reality): Same person, same bathroom — but the eyeliner is crooked, foundation doesn't match, they've stabbed themselves in the eye with the mascara wand. Both sides play in sync — same movements, different results. The Expectation side has soft lighting and a filter. The Reality side has harsh fluorescent lighting and no filter. Both people look at the camera at the end: left = confident, right = resigned.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Classic expectation vs. reality comedy. The synchronized movements make the comparison funny. The lighting difference is the meta-joke about social media filters.

**Variations:**
- Cooking: recipe video vs. kitchen disaster
- Exercise: gym routine vs. collapsing after 5 seconds
- DIY: Pinterest result vs. Pinterest fail

### Cat Logic
**Prompt:** A cat sits on a laptop keyboard while their owner tries to work. The owner gently moves the cat. The cat walks in a circle and sits back on the keyboard. The owner moves the cat again. The cat sits on the owner's lap instead — but stretches out to cover the keyboard with one paw. Owner gives up, closes the laptop. The cat immediately moves to sit on the closed laptop. Owner pulls out a decoy laptop. Cat ignores it. Cat sits on a piece of paper instead. The paper is blank. The cat looks at the camera with supreme satisfaction. Owner stares into the distance, contemplating life choices.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Universal cat owner comedy. The escalating persistence of the cat is the joke. The decoy laptop twist is the punchline. The cat's stare into camera breaks the fourth wall.

**Variations:**
- Dog version: dog brings increasingly larger objects to play with during a meeting
- Bird: parrot repeats embarrassing things during a phone call
- Baby: same as cat logic, but with a toddler

### The Group Chat
**Prompt:** Five phone screens shown simultaneously in a grid. Each person is typing a message. Messages appear one by one: "Who's free tonight?" → "I'm down" → "What time?" → "Where?" → "I'm in" → then someone types "I can't, I have [long message]" — the typing indicator shows they're still typing after everyone else has stopped. The long message finally appears: "I have to wash my hair tonight lol" — everyone's avatar goes silent. The typing indicators disappear one by one. A single person responds with "💀" emoji. Then chaos: everyone starts typing at once, messages flying. The final message: "See you at 8."

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Relatable group chat comedy. The long pause before the excuse is the tension. The skull emoji is the reaction. Everyone has been in this chat. Highly shareable.

**Variations:**
- Planning a dinner: everyone suggests different restaurants, nobody decides
- Birthday planning: one person does all the work, everyone else says "let me know"
- Work Slack: formal at first, then someone sends a meme, everything derails

## ASMR / Satisfying

### Soap Cutting
**Prompt:** Extreme close-up of a sharp knife cutting through a bar of handmade soap. The blade slices through slowly — each cut creates a perfectly smooth surface. The soap curls away in thin, translucent sheets. The texture is visible: embedded lavender buds, oat flecks, color swirls (purple, white, sage). The knife makes a satisfying whisper with each cut. The camera is at cutting-board level, macro lens, focused on the blade-soap contact point. Natural lighting from a window. The soap pieces stack neatly to the side. Each cut is precise, methodical, satisfying.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Pure ASMR satisfaction. The soap cutting is hypnotic. The macro detail of the soap's internal texture is the bonus. Clean, simple, endlessly watchable.

**Variations:**
- Kinetic sand: cutting, molding, crumbling in slow motion
- Slime: stretching, poking, bubbles forming and popping
- Wax: peeling off in perfect sheets, revealing the surface below

### Ice Cream Scoop
**Prompt:** An ice cream scoop presses into a tub of artisan gelato — pistachio green. The scoop rotates, creating a perfect sphere. The ice cream's texture is visible: creamy, dense, with pistachio pieces. The scoop lifts — the ice cream detaches from the tub with a satisfying "pop." It's placed on a waffle cone. The surface of the scoop catches light — slight melting on the edges, tiny ice crystals visible. Toppings fall from above: crushed pistachios, a drizzle of honey, a single edible flower. Each topping lands in slow motion. The cone is held up against a pastel background.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Satisfying food ASMR. The scoop's rotation and the "pop" of detachment are the sensory peaks. The topping sequence adds color and texture. Hungry-making content.

**Variations:**
- Matcha soft serve: the swirl pattern, green on white cone
- Rolled ice cream: the scraping and rolling process on a cold plate
- Affogato: espresso poured over ice cream, melting in real-time

### Perfect Loop — Marble Run
**Prompt:** A glass marble is released at the top of an elaborate Rube Goldberg machine made of wood, metal, and string. The marble rolls down a ramp, hits a domino chain, which tips a lever, which launches the marble onto a second ramp. The marble spirals through a funnel, rolls across a xylophone (creating a melody), bounces off a trampoline, swings on a tiny rope swing, and lands exactly where it started — at the top of the first ramp. The machine resets. The marble starts again. Perfect loop. Camera: slightly overhead, following the marble. The machine is handmade, imperfect, charming.

**Settings:** 1:1 (square), 4 seconds (smooth loop)

**Expected result:** Satisfying mechanical chain reaction that loops perfectly. Each step is a miniature "will it work?" moment. The loop makes it endlessly rewatchable. Instagram/TikTok loop bait.

**Variations:**
- Domino spiral: thousands of dominoes falling in a spiral pattern, looping
- Water flow: marble runs through a water-powered mill system
- Mouse trap game: the classic board game setup, ball bearing version

## Nature & Science

### Flower Blooming Time-Lapse
**Prompt:** Time-lapse of a peony blooming. The bud is tight, green, compact. Over 10 seconds (compressed from days), the petals unfurl one layer at a time. Outer petals open first — pale pink, then deeper pink as inner layers reveal themselves. The center: golden stamens emerge last, dusted with pollen. A bee arrives at the fully-open bloom, landing on the stamens. The camera is macro, static, shooting from slightly above. The background is a soft green garden bokeh. The light changes from dawn to midday as the flower opens — warm to bright.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds

**Expected result:** Nature's most satisfying content format — a flower blooming. The peony is the best subject: many layers, dramatic opening. The bee arrival is the perfect ending.

**Variations:**
- Rose: classic spiral opening, red petals
- Sunflower: turns to follow the sun as it blooms
- Corpse flower: dramatic, rare, unusual color

### Underwater Bubble Chain
**Prompt:** A chain of air bubbles rises from the bottom of a swimming pool. Each bubble is a perfect sphere — they vary in size from a marble to a grapefruit. As they rise, they wobble, merge, split. Sunlight refracts through each bubble, creating rainbow caustics on the pool floor. The camera follows the bubbles upward. At the surface, each bubble pops with a tiny splash crown — visible in slow motion. The final, largest bubble reaches the surface and creates a satisfying splash. The camera goes underwater again. New bubbles form. Smooth loop.

**Settings:** 9:16, 4 seconds (smooth loop)

**Expected result:** Satisfying physics visualization. The bubble physics are mesmerizing — wobble, merge, pop. The rainbow caustics add visual richness. Perfect loop potential.

**Variations:**
- Champagne bubbles: tiny, constant, in a glass, much smaller scale
- Boiling water: bubbles forming on a pot surface, steam rising
- Diving: person exhales bubbles, face visible through the bubble curtain

## Tips: Pika-Specific Prompt Strengths

### What Pika Does Best
- **Short clips (3-4 seconds)**: Pika excels at brief, punchy content
- **Style consistency**: Good at maintaining a consistent art style
- **Loop creation**: Can create smooth loops for social media
- **Image-to-video**: Strong at animating still images
- **Effects and edits**: Good at adding motion to existing content

### Optimal Pika Prompt Length
- **Short and focused**: 1-3 sentences is the sweet spot
- **One action per prompt**: "A cat jumps off a table" beats "A cat jumps, then runs, then sits"
- **Be specific about camera**: "Top-down shot" or "close-up" or "tracking shot"
- **Name the style**: "Anime style" or "cinematic" or "3D render"

### Aspect Ratios for Social Media
- **9:16** — TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (vertical video)
- **1:1** — Instagram feed posts, Facebook feed
- **16:9** — YouTube, Twitter/X, landscape video
- **4:5** — Instagram feed (optimized for mobile)

### Common Pika Patterns
- "A [subject] [action] in [style]" — clean and effective
- "Close-up of [subject] with [detail]" — macro detail
- "[Subject] transforming into [something else]" — morph content
- "Smooth loop of [repeating action]" — for social media
