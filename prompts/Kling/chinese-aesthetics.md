## 山水墨画 (Ink Wash Landscape)

### 千里江山图 — A Thousand Miles of Rivers and Mountains
**Prompt:** A living ink wash painting (水墨动画) of a Chinese mountain landscape. Mountains rise from mist — each peak rendered in different ink densities: near mountains in deep black, distant mountains in pale gray wash. A waterfall cascades between two peaks, rendered as white negative space. Pine trees cling to cliff faces — gnarled, ancient, each one a character. A small boat drifts on a river below — a single figure with a conical hat, fishing. The camera moves forward through the painting at a slow, steady pace — as if unrolling a scroll. The ink textures are visible: wet ink bleeding into rice paper, dry brush strokes on mountain faces, splatter techniques for foliage. The color palette: black ink, gray wash, white paper, with occasional touches of mineral blue-green (青绿) on the peaks.

**Settings:** 2.39:1 (horizontal scroll), 8 seconds

**Expected result:** A living 千里江山图 — the most famous Chinese landscape painting brought to life. The scroll-unrolling camera movement is traditional. The ink wash textures are authentic. The scale is vast and contemplative.

**Variations:**
- 富春山居图: different mountain formation, more detailed, autumn colors
- 雪景寒林图: snow-covered mountains, bare trees, monochrome
- 潇湘图: rivers and lakes, reed boats, soft atmosphere

### 墨竹图 — Ink Bamboo in Wind
**Prompt:** Close-up of bamboo painted in Chinese ink wash style. The bamboo stalks are rendered in single confident strokes — each node visible, each section slightly different. Leaves are painted in rapid, precise strokes — groupings of leaves catching different angles of light. The bamboo sways in wind — the movement is gentle, rhythmic, not dramatic. Rain begins to fall — ink-wash rain streaks appear on the painting surface. Water droplets gather on bamboo leaves, then fall. The painting is alive: the ink seems to move, to breathe. The background: rice paper texture, slightly yellowed with age. A red seal stamp (印章) is visible in the corner — the only color in the composition.

**Settings:** 9:16 (vertical scroll), 8 seconds

**Expected result:** The quintessential subject of Chinese ink painting, animated. The brushstrokes are visible and confident. The rain adds atmosphere and movement. The red seal is the finishing touch. 文人画 (literati painting) energy.

**Variations:**
- 梅花 (plum blossom): bare winter branch with blossoms, snow
- 兰花 (orchid): elegant curve, single bloom, minimal composition
- 菊花 (chrysanthemum): autumn, golden petals among ink leaves

### 山中云海 — Cloud Sea Between Mountains
**Prompt:** Aerial view of mountains emerging from a sea of clouds (云海) at dawn. The camera is above the cloud layer — the clouds are a white, undulating surface. Mountain peaks pierce through like islands — dark green against white. The sun rises from the east — the cloud surface turns from gray to pink to gold. The light moves across the cloud sea like a wave. Between two peaks, a gap in the clouds reveals a valley far below — a tiny river, a village, a bridge. The scale is vast: we are 5,000 feet above the valley. A red-crowned crane (丹顶鹤) flies through the gap between peaks, its white body briefly merging with the clouds before reappearing. The camera: slow forward movement, slight descent toward the cloud surface.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** The 云海 (cloud sea) — the most desired view in Chinese mountain culture. The scale is epic, the light is divine, and the crane adds a symbolic element (longevity, wisdom). The hidden valley below adds depth.

**Variations:**
- 黄山 (Huangshan): granite peaks, famous pine trees, specific to the location
- 张家界 (Zhangjiajie): pillar-like mountains, mist, "Avatar" inspiration
- 庐山 (Lushan): waterfall visible through clouds, poetry-inspiring

## 古风建筑 (Traditional Architecture)

### 苏州园林 — Suzhou Garden in Rain
**Prompt:** A moon gate (月洞门) in a Suzhou classical garden frames the view: a rockery (假山), a lotus pond, and a covered walkway (廊). Rain falls — each drop creates concentric ripples in the pond. The lotus leaves catch and funnel the rainwater. Koi fish (gold and white) swim beneath the surface. The garden walls are white-washed — rain streaks create ink-wash patterns on the surface. The tiles (青瓦) on the walkway glisten. A scholar in traditional hanfu (汉服) walks slowly along the corridor, holding an oil-paper umbrella (油纸伞). The camera: through the moon gate, static, watching the scene unfold. The garden is a painting composed of borrowed scenery (借景) — every element frames another.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** The Chinese garden as a living painting. The moon gate frames the scene like a scroll painting. Rain adds atmosphere and sound. The scholar adds scale and narrative. 苏州园林 (Suzhou garden) aesthetics at their finest.

**Variations:**
- Autumn: red maple reflected in the pond, fallen leaves on stepping stones
- Winter: snow on rocks, frozen pond edge, bare plum tree
- Night: lantern light, moonlight on water, fireflies in summer

### 紫禁城 — Forbidden City at Dawn
**Prompt:** The Hall of Supreme Harmony (太和殿) at dawn. The camera is positioned at the Meridian Gate (午门), looking across the vast courtyard toward the golden-roofed hall. The courtyard is empty — no tourists, no guards. The red walls glow in the first light. The yellow glazed tiles (琉璃瓦) catch the sun — they transition from deep gold to brilliant yellow. The white marble terrace (汉白玉) reflects the sky. Dragon carvings on the balustrades cast long shadows. A single bird (a magpie — 喜鹊, symbol of good fortune) lands on the golden roof ridge. The camera: wide, static, symmetrical. The composition is perfectly balanced — as it was designed to be 600 years ago. The scale: human → courtyard → hall → sky.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** The Forbidden City at its most powerful — empty, symmetrical, bathed in golden light. The human scale vs. the imperial scale is the point. The magpie adds life and cultural symbolism. 天子 (Son of Heaven) architecture.

**Variations:**
- Night: lantern-lit, ceremonial, guards at attention
- Winter: snow on the golden roofs, red walls with white contrast
- Imperial procession: figures in period costume, ceremonial flags

### 江南水镇 — Jiangnan Water Town
**Prompt:** A canal in a Jiangnan (江南) water town — Wuzhen or Xitang style. Black-tiled, white-washed buildings reflect in the still canal water. A stone bridge (石拱桥) arches over the canal — its reflection creates a perfect circle in the water. A wooden boat (乌篷船) passes under the bridge, propelled by a rower with a single oar. Red lanterns (灯笼) hang from the eaves, their reflections creating red streaks on the water surface. Morning mist softens the distance. The camera: water level, following the boat slowly. The buildings are old — wooden shutters, stone foundations, laundry hanging from second-floor windows. A cat watches from a window ledge. The only sound: the oar in the water, distant erhu music.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** The quintessential 江南 (south of the Yangtze) water town. The reflections, the stone bridge circle, the red lanterns — every element is a Chinese painting cliché because it's true. The boat adds gentle motion. Nostalgia for a disappearing China.

**Variations:**
- Night: lantern reflections dominating, warm glow on dark water
- Festival: dragon boat racing, drums, crowds on the bridges
- Winter: frost on the stone bridge, breath visible, different quietness

## 传统文化 (Traditional Culture)

### 茶道 — Tea Ceremony
**Prompt:** A gongfu tea ceremony (工夫茶). Close-up sequence: a Yixing clay teapot (紫砂壶) is warmed with hot water — steam rises. Tea leaves (oolong, dark, tightly rolled) are placed in the pot — each leaf visible. Hot water is poured in a circular motion — the leaves begin to unfurl. The first brew is poured over the teapot (养壶 — seasoning the pot). The second brew: amber liquid pours into small cups (品茗杯) in a circular pattern — each cup filled equally. A hand lifts a cup — fingers elegant, practiced. The tea is sipped: eyes close, breath out through the nose. The tea table (茶盘) is dark wood — water stains and tea rings mark years of use. Camera: macro, alternating between the pouring, the leaves unfurling, and the sip.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** The tea ceremony as meditation. Each step has purpose and beauty. The macro detail on the leaves unfurling and the amber liquid pouring are the visual ASMR. The aged tea table tells a story of practice.

**Variations:**
- Japanese matcha: bamboo whisk, green foam, different ritual
- Pu-erh: aged tea, dark brew, the breaking of a tea cake
- Tea house: full scene, multiple guests, conversation implied

### 书法 — Calligraphy Master at Work
**Prompt:** A calligraphy master stands before a large sheet of rice paper (宣纸) on the floor. They hold a large brush (大楷笔), loaded with ink. They study the blank paper — composing in their mind. Then they attack: the first stroke is decisive, bold, from left to right (横). The brush moves with controlled fury — each stroke has rhythm, like music. Ink splatters with the energy of the movement. The camera: overhead shot, capturing the full composition emerging. The character: 龙 (dragon) — complex, requiring multiple strokes, each one building on the last. The master's face: intense, breathing through the strokes. The finished character: alive, powerful, imperfect — the imperfection is the art. A moment of stillness. Then: the red seal stamp, pressed firmly.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Calligraphy as martial art — the master's movement has the energy of a swordsman. The overhead view shows the character emerging stroke by stroke. The red seal is the final punctuation. 书法 (calligraphy) as performance.

**Variations:**
- Small regular script (小楷): precise, meditative, seated position
- Grass script (草书): cursive, flowing, continuous motion, faster
- Water calligraphy: writing on pavement with water in a park, evaporating

### 武术 — Tai Chi at Dawn
**Prompt:** A tai chi practitioner (太极) performs the opening movements of Yang-style tai chi in a park at dawn. The movement is impossibly slow — each transition between postures takes seconds. The camera: medium shot, slightly slow-motion to emphasize the deliberateness. The practitioner's silk tai chi uniform (white) catches the breeze. Their feet are rooted — the weight shifts are visible. Hands trace arcs in the air — cloud hands (云手), the signature movement. Behind them: an ancient tree, morning mist, the sound of birdsong. The practitioner's breathing is visible — the chest rises and falls with each posture. Their expression: peaceful, focused, present. The camera slowly orbits, revealing the depth of each posture from different angles.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** The meditative beauty of tai chi made visible. The slowness IS the point — every micro-movement has purpose. The park at dawn provides the traditional setting. The silk uniform's movement adds visual grace. 太极拳 (taijiquan) as moving meditation.

**Variations:**
- Sword form (太极剑): flowing silk, blade catching light, different energy
- Push hands (推手): two practitioners, subtle power, balance disruption
- Group practice: dozens of people in a park, synchronized, community

## 武侠 (Wuxia)

### 竹林对决 — Bamboo Forest Duel
**Prompt:** Two swordsmen face each other in a bamboo forest. They stand 20 feet apart — motionless. Bamboo stalks create a natural arena around them. Wind moves through the bamboo — the stalks creak and sway. A single bamboo leaf falls between them. The leaf touches the ground. Both draw simultaneously — blades ring. The camera: split-second cuts between close-ups — eyes, hands on hilts, blade reflections. One swordsman leaps — the camera follows in slow-motion as they arc through the air, bamboo stalks parting around the blade. The other parries — sparks fly. The force of the impact: bamboo stalks snap and fall in a cascade. The two face each other again. A scratch appears on one swordsman's cheek — a single drop of blood. They smile. Respect. They sheathe their swords. The duel is over. Nobody won.

**Settings:** 2.39:1 (wuxia widescreen), 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Classic wuxia duel — slow-motion, bamboo forest, philosophical undertones. The falling leaf triggers the action. The bamboo destruction shows the force. The mutual smile ends the conflict with honor. 张艺谋 (Zhang Yimou) "Hero" aesthetic.

**Variations:**
- Snow duel: white ground, red blood, black robes — color contrast
- Water duel: standing on water (wire work), splashing, reflections
- Cherry blossom duel: petals falling with each sword strike, poetic

### 轻功 — Lightness Skill (Qinggong)
**Prompt:** A martial artist in flowing white robes runs across the surface of a lake. Each footstep touches the water — creating a circular ripple — before lifting again. The movement is impossible but rendered with wuxia logic: speed, lightness, qi (气). The camera: side-on tracking shot, keeping the martial artist in the center of frame. Their robe trails behind them, touching the water. In the distance: a mountain temple (寺庙) perched on a cliff edge. The martial artist reaches the shore — they don't slow down but leap onto a tree branch, then another, ascending the cliff face in a series of impossible jumps. They arrive at the temple gate. Land softly. Not a drop of water on their robes. The camera: pulls back to reveal the vast lake behind them, the trail of ripples still visible.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** The wuxia fantasy made visual — running on water, lightness skill, defying gravity. The rippling water trail proves the impossibility. The dry robes at the end are the final flex. Every wuxia reader's dream visualization.

**Variations:**
- Wall running: up the side of a building, defying gravity, cape flowing
- Tree-top travel: leaping between branches, effortless, bird-like
- Roof-running: across ceramic tile roofs, village below, moonlit

### 内功 — Internal Energy Visualization
**Prompt:** A martial arts master sits in meditation (打坐) in a mountain cave. Their hands rest on their knees, palms up. A faint golden light (气, qi) begins to glow at their lower abdomen (丹田, dantian). The light grows — it's visible through their clothing. It flows upward through their body: along the meridians (经络), tracing paths along the arms, the spine, the crown. The camera: close-up, slowly orbiting the seated figure. The qi energy extends beyond the body now — a golden aura, pulsing with their heartbeat. Small stones on the cave floor begin to levitate. The master's hair flows upward. Water droplets in the cave reverse direction — falling upward. The master exhales — a visible breath, carrying golden particles. They open their eyes: they glow, briefly, then return to normal. The stones settle. Calm.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Internal energy (内功) made visible. The golden qi flowing through meridian paths is the visual concept. The environmental effects (floating stones, reversed water) show the power's reach. The brief eye glow is the punctuation mark.

**Variations:**
- Healing: green energy flowing to a wound, closing, skin knitting
- Combat qi: red energy, explosive, punching shockwaves through air
- Balance: yin-yang energy, black and white, dual circulation

## 诗词意境 (Poetic Atmosphere)

### 枫桥夜泊 — Night Mooring at Maple Bridge
**Prompt:** The poem by 张继 (Zhang Ji) made visual: a lone boat moored under a maple bridge (枫桥) at night. The moon sets — low on the horizon, painting the sky in deep indigo. Frost fills the air — visible as a fine white mist. Maple trees line the riverbank — their leaves are red, but in the moonlight they appear black-red. A crow (乌鸦) calls — its silhouette crosses the moon. In the distance: the Hanshan Temple (寒山寺). Its bell tower is lit by a single lantern. The bell rings — the camera captures the sound as visible ripples expanding through the frost-filled air. The boat rocks gently. A scholar lies in the boat, unable to sleep, watching the sky. The poem's mood: 旅愁 (traveler's sorrow), isolation, beauty in melancholy.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Classical Tang Dynasty poetry rendered as video. Every element comes from the poem — the maple bridge, the setting moon, the crow, the temple bell, the frost. The mood is 旅愁 — the lonely beauty of being far from home. 诗中有画 (poetry contains painting).

**Variations:**
- 静夜思 (Quiet Night Thought): moonlight through a window, homesickness
- 春江花月夜 (Spring River Flower Moon Night): river, flowers, moon, all in one frame
- 山行 (Mountain Walk): autumn mountain path, red leaves, white clouds

### 清明 — Qingming Festival
**Prompt:** A scene inspired by 清明上河图 (Along the River During the Qingming Festival). A Song Dynasty (宋朝) city street at festival time. The camera: slow dolly along a bustling commercial street. Shops on both sides: a noodle vendor (steam from the pot), a fabric merchant (bolt of silk unrolled), a medicine shop (herbs hanging from the ceiling). People in period clothing: scholars, merchants, laborers, children. A horse-drawn cart navigates the crowded street. A bridge in the background: arching over a canal, lined with vendors. Boats on the canal below. The details: a dog sleeping in a doorway, a child buying candy, a calligrapher writing a sign. The light: overcast, spring, gentle. The style: Zhang Zeduan's painting, but alive — colors natural, movement natural, life happening.

**Settings:** 2.39:1 (epic widescreen), 8 seconds

**Expected result:** China's most famous painting, animated — Song Dynasty daily life brought to life. The street-level detail is the point: every person has a story, every shop has a purpose. The bridge is the visual anchor. 清明上河图 meets living history.

**Variations:**
- Canal section: boats, loading docks, fishermen, the river perspective
- Night market: lantern-lit, different energy, food vendors, performers
- Seasons: same street in winter, summer, autumn — different activities

### 桃花源 — Peach Blossom Spring
**Prompt:** The Tao Yuanming (陶渊明) poem made visual: a fisherman follows a stream through a narrow mountain pass (桃花林 — peach blossom forest). The peach trees are in full bloom — petals fall like pink snow, covering the water surface. The camera: follows the fisherman from behind as he pushes through hanging vines and emerges into the hidden valley (桃花源). Inside: a perfect village. Rice paddies in geometric patterns. Farmers working with water buffalo. Children playing. Smoke rising from thatched roofs. The light: perpetual golden hour, impossibly warm and perfect. The villagers look up — surprised, but welcoming. The fisherman stands at the entrance, awestruck. Petals continue to fall. The valley is timeless — there are no seasons here, no war, no age. The camera pulls back: the mountain pass closes, the valley is hidden again. Was it real?

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** China's most beloved utopian myth visualized. The transition from the peach blossom forest to the hidden valley is the magic. The golden light and the falling petals create paradise. The final closure of the mountain pass is the bittersweet ending — you can't go back.

**Variations:**
- Discovery: the fisherman following the stream, finding the narrow pass
- Village life: close-up of the village — cooking, weaving, playing music
- Return attempt: the fisherman trying to find the valley again, failing — only peach blossoms remain

## Tips: Describing Chinese Artistic Concepts in English Prompts

### Core Aesthetic Concepts
- **意境 (yìjìng)**: "Artistic mood / atmosphere" — the emotional landscape beyond the visual. Describe the feeling, not just the scene: "a sense of solitary contemplation, like the last person awake in the world"
- **留白 (liúbái)**: "Negative space / deliberate emptiness" — "80% of the frame is empty white space, with a single small figure in the lower third"
- **气韵 (qìyùn)**: "Spirit resonance / vital energy" — "the scene has a breathing quality, as if the landscape itself is alive"
- **写意 (xiěyì)**: "Freehand / expressive style" — "loose brushwork, suggesting rather than defining, the essence captured in minimal strokes"
- **工笔 (gōngbǐ)**: "Meticulous / detailed style" — "every feather on the bird is individually rendered, every hair on the horse's mane is visible"

### Color Descriptions
- **朱砂红 (zhūshā hóng)**: "Cinnabar red — the red of temple walls and seal paste"
- **青绿 (qīnglǜ)**: "Blue-green — the mineral pigment color of classical landscape painting"
- **水墨黑 (shuǐmò hēi)**: "Ink black — ranging from dense to translucent, pure Chinese ink"
- **月白 (yuèbái)**: "Moon white — a pale blue-white, the color of moonlight on snow"
- **黛青 (dàiqīng)**: "Dark cyan-gray — the color of distant mountains"

### Nature Descriptions
- **云海 (yúnhǎi)**: "Sea of clouds — cloud inversion layer filling valleys between mountain peaks"
- **飞瀑 (fēipù)**: "Flying waterfall — a waterfall that appears to fall from heaven"
- **奇松 (qísōng)**: "Wonderful pines — ancient, gnarled pine trees growing from impossible positions on cliffs"
- **怪石 (guàishí)**: "Strange rocks — unusual rock formations, often limestone, shaped by millennia of erosion"

### Architectural Terms
- **亭 (tíng)**: "Chinese pavilion — open-sided, with upturned eaves"
- **廊 (láng)**: "Covered walkway / corridor connecting buildings"
- **月洞门 (yuèdòngmén)**: "Moon gate — circular doorway in garden walls"
- **飞檐 (fēiyán)**: "Flying eaves — the upturned roof corners of traditional Chinese buildings"
- **斗拱 (dǒugǒng)**: "Bracket set — the interlocking wooden brackets supporting the roof"
