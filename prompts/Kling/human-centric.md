## Dance

### Street Dance Freestyle
**Prompt:** A street dancer in a warehouse space performs a freestyle routine. The dancer wears loose joggers and a white t-shirt. The movement starts small — a head nod, a shoulder roll — then expands into full-body popping and locking. Each pop is precise: the arm freezes mid-air, then the chest pops, then the other arm. The warehouse has natural light from skylights above — the dancer moves through patches of light and shadow. Dust particles float in the light beams. Camera: medium shot, slight low angle, tracking the dancer's movement. The dancer finishes with a freeze — balanced on one hand, body horizontal. Hold the pose.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** Authentic street dance with visible control and precision. Kling's strength with human motion makes the popping mechanics believable. The dust particles and warehouse light add production value.

**Variations:**
- Breaking: windmills, power moves, floor work
- Contemporary: fluid, emotional, reaching movements
- Ballroom: two dancers, waltz, flowing dress, spinning

### Ballet Rehearsal
**Prompt:** A ballet dancer in a studio performs a grand jeté (split leap). The camera captures the full arc — the run-up, the launch, the peak of the jump (legs in full split, arms extended), and the landing. The studio has floor-to-ceiling mirrors reflecting the movement. Natural light from large windows. The dancer's pointe shoes are worn — satin frayed, ribbons slightly loose. Their face shows concentration, not performance. The camera: side profile, tracking the leap, slightly slow-motion (60fps). After the landing: the dancer adjusts their bun, walks back to their starting position, and prepares to do it again.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** Technical ballet captured with respect for the art form. The leap's arc is the hero moment. The worn shoes and studio setting add authenticity. Not glamorous — real.

**Variations:**
- Pirouette: multiple rotations, spotting technique visible, fouetté turns
- Pas de deux: partner lift, trust and strength, overhead press
- Barre work: morning class, pliés and tendus, quiet discipline

### K-Pop Choreography
**Prompt:** Five dancers in a practice room perform K-pop choreography in perfect synchronization. The moves are sharp: arm isolations, body waves, footwork patterns, formation changes. They shift from a V-formation to a line to scattered positions. The practice room has wood flooring, mirrored walls, and fluorescent lighting. The dancers wear matching black practice clothes. The camera: front-facing, medium-wide, capturing the full formation. Every move is identical across all five dancers — sync is the point. A formation change: two dancers slide left while three slide right. They meet again in the center.

**Settings:** 9:16, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** The satisfying precision of synchronized dance. Formation changes are the visual highlight. The practice room setting makes it feel like a real rehearsal, not a performance.

**Variations:**
- Solo practice: one dancer in the same room, drilling the same moves
- Dance cover: different music, same choreography, casual clothes
- Behind-the-scenes: one dancer teaching the others, laughing at mistakes

## Sports

### Basketball Trick Shot
**Prompt:** A basketball player stands at half court. They spin the ball on one finger — the ball's rotation is visible, seams blurring. They stop the spin, bounce the ball once (the bounce echoes in the empty gym), and shoot. The ball arcs through the air — camera follows its trajectory in slow motion. The ball hits the backboard, bounces off the rim, circles the rim once (the player watches, holding their breath), and drops through the net. The net swishes — every strand is visible in slow motion. The player's reaction: a fist pump, then a casual shrug — they do this all the time. Empty gym, hardwood floor, overhead lights creating pools of light.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** The perfect trick shot moment. The ball's journey is the suspense — the rim circling is the peak tension. The shrug at the end is the character beat. Gym acoustics implied.

**Variations:**
- Soccer: free kick, curve shot, top corner, net bulging
- Tennis: serve, ball flattening on impact, chalk flying from the line
- Skateboarding: kickflip, board rotation, clean landing, rolling away

### Martial Arts Kata
**Prompt:** A martial artist performs a kata (forms) in a dojo. Each movement is precise: strikes snap to full extension, blocks are solid, stances are deep and rooted. The gi (uniform) makes sharp sounds with each movement — the cotton snapping. The camera orbits the martial artist slowly, capturing different angles of the same kata. A single shaft of sunlight from a high window illuminates dust in the air. The martial artist's breathing is visible — controlled, rhythmic. The final move: a powerful punch held at full extension. The camera pushes in on the fist — knuckles white, veins visible, trembling slightly with the effort. Then relax. A bow.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** The beauty of martial arts as meditation in motion. Each technique has purpose and precision. The dojo setting and natural light create a sacred space feeling. The trembling fist at the end shows human effort.

**Variations:**
- Wushu: flowing, acrobatic, different energy — more performance than combat
- Tai chi: slow, continuous, circular movements, outdoor setting
- Capoeira: rhythmic, acrobatic, music-driven, roda circle

### Swimming Underwater
**Prompt:** Underwater shot of a competitive swimmer doing a butterfly stroke. The camera is at pool level, filming through the water. The swimmer's dolphin kick is visible — the undulation travels from chest through hips to feet. Each arm pull creates a trail of bubbles. The swimmer's face breaks the surface — water sheeting off their goggles, mouth gasping for air, then submerging again. The pool water is crystal blue, lit by overhead competition lights refracting through the water. Lane lines create a repeating pattern in the background. The swimmer's technique is efficient — minimal splash, maximum propulsion. The camera: side-on, tracking at swimming speed.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** The grace of competitive swimming, seen from below the waterline. The dolphin kick's undulation is hypnotic. The air-to-water transition creates drama. Clean, athletic, beautiful.

**Variations:**
- Diver: entry into water, minimal splash, underwater descent
- Surfer: riding a wave, spray, balance, the curl of the wave
- Synchronized swimming: underwater formations, perfectly timed

## Cooking & Craft

### Hand-Pulled Noodles
**Prompt:** A noodle master in a Beijing restaurant makes hand-pulled noodles (拉面). Close-up on flour-dusted hands as they stretch and fold the dough — each pull doubles the strand count. The dough transforms from a thick rope to impossibly thin noodles in a series of rhythmic stretches and slaps against the counter. Flour flies with each slap. The master's face is focused, sweat on their brow. The final pull: the noodles are gossamer-thin, translucent, nearly a hundred strands held in two hands. They're tossed into boiling water — the water erupts with bubbles. Steam rises. Camera: alternating close-ups on hands and medium shots of the technique.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** Mesmerizing craft demonstration. The transformation from thick dough to thin noodles is the visual magic. Each pull and slap has rhythm. The steam and boiling water add sensory depth.

**Variations:**
- Pizza dough toss: spinning dough in the air, stretching, flour dust
- Ramen preparation: broth ladling, noodle cooking, assembly
- Dumpling making: precise folding, pleating, bamboo steamer

### Woodworking Hand Plane
**Prompt:** Close-up of a craftsman using a Japanese hand plane (kanna) on a piece of hinoki (cypress) wood. The plane moves toward camera in a single, smooth stroke. A curl of wood shaving — paper-thin, translucent — rises from the blade. The shaving catches light — it's so thin you can almost see through it. The wood surface is revealed: perfectly smooth, the grain visible in warm golden tones. The craftsman's hand is steady, calloused, experienced. They blow away sawdust and inspect the surface — running their fingertips along the grain. The camera: macro, focused on the blade-wood contact point. Natural workshop lighting.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** ASMR-level craftsmanship. The paper-thin shaving is the proof of skill. The wood grain reveal is the reward. Japanese craft aesthetics — precision, patience, beauty in function.

**Variations:**
- Pottery wheel: clay forming between hands, spinning, becoming a bowl
- Leather working: stitching by hand, needle and thread, waxed cord
- Glassblowing: molten glass on a pipe, shaping, glowing orange

### Calligraphy Stroke
**Prompt:** A calligrapher holds a loaded brush (毛笔) over rice paper. The brush descends — contact. Ink spreads into the paper fibers (visible at macro level). The stroke begins: thick (pressing down), curves (rolling the brush), thins (lifting), and ends with a decisive flick (提). The character: 永 (yǒng, "eternity") — the fundamental character containing all eight basic strokes of Chinese calligraphy. Each stroke follows: horizontal, vertical, left-falling, right-falling, dot, hook, rising, turning. The brush moves with confidence — there's no hesitation, no correction. Each stroke is final. Camera: overhead, macro, watching the ink spread with each contact.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** The art of Chinese calligraphy as performance. The ink's spread into paper is visible at macro level. The character 永 is the most fundamental in calligraphy training — it contains everything. Each stroke is permanent.

**Variations:**
- Seals: red seal paste stamping, the impression appearing
- Water calligraphy: writing on pavement with water, evaporating
- Ink grinding: grinding ink stick on stone, black ink pooling

## Music & Performance

### Drum Solo
**Prompt:** A drummer behind a full kit performs a solo. Close-up sequence: sticks hit the snare — the skin ripples with each strike. Hi-hat — the cymbal opens and closes, creating the characteristic "chick." Tom fill — the stick travels across three toms in a single fluid motion. Crash cymbal — it vibrates, shimmering. Kick drum — the beater hits the skin, the pedal compresses. The camera cuts between close-ups in rhythm with the drumming. The drummer's face: intense focus, then a grin when a particularly satisfying fill lands. Sweat flies from their hair on each head movement. The kit: classic, well-worn, stickered — this isn't a showroom kit, it's a working musician's instrument.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** The physicality of drumming made cinematic. Each hit has visual impact — cymbal vibrations, skin ripples, sweat droplets. The camera cuts create rhythm even without sound.

**Variations:**
- Piano: fingers on keys, hammers hitting strings (shown transparently)
- Guitar: close-up of fretboard, string bending, pick attack
- Violin: bow drawing across strings, rosin dust, vibrato

### Street Performer — Violin
**Prompt:** A street violinist plays in a subway station. The camera is close — focused on the violin and bow. The bow draws across the strings in long, emotional strokes. Rosin dust particles are visible in the station's fluorescent light. The violinist's fingers on the fingerboard: vibrato, shifting positions, precise intonation. The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the subway station: commuters walking past, some slowing down, a child stopping to watch. Coins in the open case. The violinist closes their eyes — lost in the music. A train arrives in the background — doors open, people exit, new commuters pass. The violinist plays on, unheard by most, seen by few.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** The lonely beauty of street performance. The rosin dust in artificial light is the visual hook. The contrast between the musician's absorption and the commuters' indifference is the narrative.

**Variations:**
- Jazz saxophone: outdoor night, street lamp, smoke, blue notes
- Beatboxer: close-up on mouth, different sounds visible
- DJ: turntable, vinyl spinning, hand scratching, crowd reaction

### Choir Rehearsal
**Prompt:** A choir of 20 singers in a church rehearsal space. The conductor raises their hands — silence. The downbeat. Singers breathe together (visible chest expansion in sync), then sound begins — mouths opening, faces showing the effort of breath support. Camera moves through the choir at eye level: each face tells a different story of concentration and feeling. Sopranos: eyes up, mouths open wide. Basses: deep breathing, chins down. Altos: focused, mid-register. A tenor's voice cracks slightly — they recover instantly. The conductor's hands shape the sound: soft, loud, crescendo. The final chord: everyone holds, the sound fills the space, the conductor's hands tremble with the effort of holding the cutoff.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** The collective human experience of singing together. Individual faces in a group — each one concentrating, feeling, breathing. The conductor's trembling hands at the cutoff is the emotional beat.

**Variations:**
- Gospel choir: more energy, movement, clapping, call-and-response
- A cappella group: small, tight harmonies, circle formation
- Children's choir: different energy, innocence, small voices in a big space

## Daily Life & Emotion

The person wraps both hands around the mug. Close-up on the hands: warmth creating a micro-expression of comfort. They sip. Eyes close. The window behind them shows it's still dark outside — 5:47 AM on the clock. This is before the world wakes up. The camera: intimate, close, observing a private moment.

**Settings:** 16:9, 5 seconds

**Expected result:** The sacred morning ritual that millions share. Every sensory detail is visual: the sound of grinding, the heat of the mug, the taste in the eyes-closed sip. The early hour adds solitude.

**Variations:**
- Tea ceremony: matcha whisking, bowl warming, precise movements
- Breakfast cooking: eggs frying, toast popping, plate assembly
- Journaling: pen on paper, morning light, steam from the mug

### Elderly Couple Walking
**Prompt:** An elderly couple walks hand-in-hand down a tree-lined path in autumn. Their pace is slow — they match each other's steps perfectly. Leaves fall around them. The man adjusts the woman's scarf. She pats his hand. They don't speak — they don't need to. They've walked this path before: their rhythm is synced by decades. A bench comes into view — they sit, still holding hands. The woman leans her head on his shoulder. He looks at her, then at the path ahead. The camera: wide shot from behind, slowly pushing in. **Expected result:** A love story told in 5 seconds. No dialogue, no drama — just the accumulated intimacy of decades expressed in synchronized steps. The scarf adjustment says "I love you" more than words. Devastating in its quietness.

**Variations:**

## Tips: Kling's Strengths with Human Motion

### Why Kling Excels at Humans
- **Hand fidelity**: Kling maintains realistic hand proportions and movements (a common AI weakness)
- **Facial micro-expressions**: Subtle expressions — a twitch, a blink, a breath — are more natural
- **Body mechanics**: Walking, running, jumping follow realistic physics
- **Clothing interaction**: Fabric moves with the body, not independently
- **Group dynamics**: Multiple people moving in sync is handled well

### Optimizing Prompts for Human Subjects
- **Name the action precisely**: "A grand jeté" not "a jump"
- **Describe body mechanics**: "The weight shifts to the left foot, the right leg extends"
- **Include clothing details**: "A silk dress that flows with the movement" — Kling handles fabric well
- **Specify hand positions**: "The right hand holds the brush at a 45-degree angle"
- **Add micro-movements**: "A slight head tilt," "the eyes narrow," "breath visible in cold air"

### What Kling Handles Best
- Dance (all styles) — natural strength
- Sports and athletics — good body mechanics
- Cooking and craft — excellent with hand work
- Musical performance — instrument handling is realistic
- Daily life actions — walking, sitting, gesturing are natural

### Common Pitfalls
- Requesting very fast motion: Kling handles deliberate movement better than rapid action
- Multiple complex actions in one prompt: focus on one main action
- Extreme camera angles: overhead and low-angle work, but extreme Dutch angles can be inconsistent
- Very long prompts: keep descriptions focused on the human action, not the entire environment
