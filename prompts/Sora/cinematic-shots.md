## Landscape Establishing Shots

### Golden Hour Mountain Valley
**Prompt:** A slow helicopter push-in over a vast glacial valley at golden hour. The camera descends gradually from 500ft, revealing a turquoise river winding through autumn foliage — amber, crimson, burnt orange. Volumetric god rays pierce through scattered cumulus clouds. Shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with a 21mm Ultra Prime. Film grain subtle, color graded in the style of Roger Deakins.

**Settings:** 16:9, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Sweeping cinematic landscape with rich warm tones, god rays visible in the valley, smooth camera descent revealing the river below.

**Variations:**
- Replace autumn foliage with snow-covered pine forest for a winter version
- Shift to blue hour (pre-dawn) with deep indigo tones and no god rays
- Add a single eagle soaring through the frame at 0:12

### Desert Highway Drone Shot
**Prompt:** Aerial tracking shot following a lone black SUV driving down a perfectly straight desert highway that stretches to the horizon. Cracked salt flats on both sides. Heat haze distorts the road ahead. Late afternoon sun casts long shadows. Camera maintains 30ft altitude, drifting slowly left. Shot in 8K, Zeiss Master Prime 14mm look. Color palette: desaturated ochre, deep shadow blacks, bleached sky.

**Settings:** 2.39:1 (anamorphic), 15 seconds

**Expected result:** Widescreen desert road shot with heat distortion, long shadows, and a sense of infinite distance. Terrence Malick / "Paris, Texas" aesthetic.

**Variations:**
- Night version: headlights only, star-filled sky, Milky Way visible
- Replace SUV with a vintage red convertible for a different mood
- Add dust storm approaching from the left side of frame

### Norwegian Fjord Dawn
**Prompt:** Ultra-wide establishing shot of a Norwegian fjord at 4:30 AM in midsummer. Camera positioned on a cliff edge, slowly tilting down to reveal mirror-still water reflecting snow-capped mountains. A single wooden fishing boat drifts in the center. Mist curls off the water surface. Color temperature shifts from deep blue to pale pink as the sun barely touches the horizon. Minimal camera movement — only a 2-degree tilt over 15 seconds. Shot on RED V-Raptor, 18mm.

**Settings:** 16:9, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Serene, almost meditative fjord shot with perfect reflections and gradual color temperature shift. Nordic noir meets paradise.

**Variations:**
- Add northern lights (aurora borealis) in the sky for a night version
- Replace stillness with gentle rainfall creating ripples on the water surface
- Winter version: frozen fjord, snow on cliffs, breath-visible cold

## City & Urban Establishing Shots

### Tokyo Neon Rain
**Prompt:** Tracking shot at street level through Shinjuku's Kabukicho district in heavy rain at night. Camera follows a person holding a transparent umbrella from behind, 3ft off the ground. Neon signs in Japanese kanji reflect off wet asphalt in pink, blue, and electric green. Puddles splash with each footstep. Steam rises from a ramen shop vent on the left. Shot on Sony Venice 2, 35mm anamorphic, shallow depth of field. Moody, Wong Kar-wai inspired color grade.

**Settings:** 2.39:1, 15 seconds

**Expected result:** Atmospheric rain-soaked Tokyo street scene with neon reflections, steam, and a sense of lonely beauty. "Fallen Angels" meets cyberpunk.

**Variations:**
- Daytime version: harsh midday sun, crowded crossings, no rain
- POV from inside a taxi looking out through rain-beaded glass
- Overhead crane shot rising above the street to reveal the full neon grid

### Manhattan Golden Hour Skyline
**Prompt:** Time-lapse of Manhattan skyline from Weehawken, NJ waterfront. Camera locked on tripod. Clouds streak eastward at 400x speed. The Empire State Building transitions from day-lit steel gray to warm amber to its signature white tower lights. Hudson River reflects the entire color shift. One minute compressed into a smooth loop. Shot at f/8, 24mm, one frame every 4 seconds played at 24fps.

**Settings:** 16:9, 15 seconds (loop)

**Expected result:** Smooth time-lapse of Manhattan transitioning from day to night with dramatic cloud movement and building illumination.

**Variations:**
- Reverse: night to dawn, city lights fading as sky brightens
- Add boat traffic on the Hudson with light trails
- Brooklyn Bridge foreground with Manhattan behind

### Old Havana Street Life
**Prompt:** Slow dolly shot (left to right) along a narrow Havana street at eye level. Pastel-colored colonial buildings with crumbling plaster — turquoise, coral, faded yellow. A vintage 1957 Chevrolet Bel Air in cherry red is parked in the foreground. Locals sit on doorsteps playing dominoes. Laundry hangs from wrought-iron balconies. A dog sleeps in a patch of shade. Late afternoon light, warm and golden. Shot on 35mm Kodak Vision3 500T film stock with natural grain. Handheld feel but stabilized.

**Settings:** 16:9, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Warm, textured street-level portrait of Havana with authentic vintage feel, rich colors, and film grain.

**Variations:**
- Night version: single streetlamp, moody shadows, salsa music implied by a lit doorway
- Rainy version: wet cobblestones, reflections of pastel walls
- Add a cyclist passing through frame from right to left

## Nature & Wildlife

### Monarch Butterfly Migration
**Prompt:** Macro tracking shot following a single monarch butterfly as it lifts off from a milkweed flower. Camera matches its flight path as it joins thousands of other monarchs filling the air like orange confetti. Dappled sunlight filters through an oyamel fir forest canopy. Background is a bokeh wall of orange wings. Shot on macro probe lens at f/2.8. Shallow depth of field. Natural ambient sound design: wing flutter, forest birdsong.

**Settings:** 16:9, 15 seconds

**Expected result:** Intimate macro-to-wide transition showing the scale of monarch migration. Beautiful bokeh, warm light, sense of wonder.

**Variations:**
- Winter version: monarchs clustered on tree trunks in dense colonies
- Ground-level shot looking up through thousands of flying monarchs against blue sky
- Add rain: butterflies sheltering under leaves, droplets on wings

### Underwater Kelp Forest
**Prompt:** Slow steadicam glide through a giant kelp forest off the California coast. Sunlight filters down through 60ft of kelp canopy, creating dancing light shafts in blue-green water. A harbor seal glides past the camera, curious, turning its head. Leopard sharks patrol the sandy bottom. Schools of sardines swirl in synchronized patterns. Camera moves forward at walking pace, 5ft off the seabed. Shot in underwater IMAX style, natural ocean lighting, no artificial light.

**Settings:** 16:9, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Immersive underwater forest experience with natural light shafts, marine life, and a sense of peaceful exploration.

**Variations:**
- Night dive version: bioluminescent plankton, dark water, single dive light
- Add a diver entering frame from above, descending through the kelp
- Macro focus on a single garibaldi fish defending its territory

### Volcanic Eruption at Dawn
**Prompt:** Telephoto shot (200mm) from 2 miles away of an active volcano erupting at dawn. Lava fountains shoot 1000ft into a dark pre-dawn sky. Pyroclastic flow streams down the mountainside like rivers of molten orange. Lightning crackles within the ash plume. The foreground is black volcanic rock with sparse dead trees silhouetted. Camera slowly pushes in over 15 seconds. Color contrast: deep blacks, molten orange, electric blue lightning. Shot on RED Monstro 8K.

**Settings:** 16:9, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Dramatic volcanic eruption with extreme color contrast between molten lava and dark landscape. Apocalyptic yet beautiful.

**Variations:**
- Aerial drone shot circling the crater at safe altitude
- Daytime version: ash plume against blue sky, less dramatic but more documentary
- Add ocean in foreground: lava flowing into water creating steam clouds

## Space & Sci-Fi

### ISS Orbit Over Earth's Terminator
**Prompt:** POV from the International Space Station cupola window. Earth rotates below at orbital speed — 17,500 mph. The terminator line (day/night boundary) slides across the frame. On the day side: deep blue ocean, swirling white cyclones, green coastlines. On the night side: city light grids — Tokyo, Seoul, Shanghai — glowing like circuit boards. Auroras shimmer at the poles in green and purple. Camera is static, Earth moves. Shot in IMAX-quality 8K, true orbital perspective, no fish-eye.

**Settings:** 16:9, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Hyper-realistic ISS window view with Earth's terminator line transition, city lights, and auroras. "Overview effect" feeling.

**Variations:**
- Moonrise over Earth's limb — lunar surface creeping into frame from below
- Meteor burning up in the atmosphere below, bright streak across the night side
- Soyuz capsule in frame, docking approach

### Asteroid Belt Mining Operation
**Prompt:** Wide establishing shot of a massive industrial mining station anchored to a tumbling asteroid. The station has rotating habitat rings, glowing furnace vents, and cargo drones shuttling ore to a waiting freighter. Background: deep space with Jupiter looming large, its Great Red Spot visible. Lighting: harsh industrial work lights on the station, soft reflected sunlight from Jupiter. Camera slowly orbits the entire assembly. Hard sci-fi aesthetic — "The Expanse" meets "Alien." Gritty, lived-in detail.

**Settings:** 2.39:1, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Detailed sci-fi industrial scene with realistic scale, harsh lighting contrast, and a sense of lonely frontier life in space.

**Variations:**
- Interior shot: workers in the habitat ring, gravity simulation visible
- Action version: an explosion rocks the mining station, debris floats
- Close-up: a maintenance drone repairs a solar panel, Earth visible as a tiny dot

### Black Hole Accretion Disk
**Prompt:** Camera slowly approaches a stellar-mass black hole. Its accretion disk glows in spiraling bands of superheated plasma — white-hot at the inner edge, cooling to deep red at the outer edge. Gravitational lensing warps the starfield behind it, creating an Einstein ring. Photon spheres create ghostly secondary images. The event horizon is a perfect circle of absolute nothing. Camera maintains safe distance, orbiting at 30 degrees inclination. Christopher Nolan's "Interstellar" scientific advisor style. No music — just the low rumble of spacetime distortion.

**Settings:** 16:9, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Scientifically accurate black hole visualization with dramatic gravitational lensing and glowing accretion disk. Awe-inspiring and terrifying.

**Variations:**
- Binary black hole system: two black holes orbiting each other, accretion disks interacting
- Add a spaceship approaching for scale reference
- Zoom into the accretion disk: plasma storms, magnetic field lines visible

## Underwater & Aquatic

### Deep Sea Bioluminescence
**Prompt:** Camera descends vertically into the deep ocean, starting from 200m depth. As darkness increases, bioluminescent organisms begin to appear — first scattered, then increasingly dense. A siphonophore colony drifts past, its segments glowing electric blue. Deep-sea jellyfish pulse in purple and green. The camera rotates slowly as it descends, creating a vortex of light particles. At 1000m, a massive sperm whale passes overhead, its silhouette blocking the faint surface light. Shot on deep-sea camera rig, no artificial light.

**Settings:** 16:9, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Otherworldly deep-sea descent through layers of bioluminescent life. Ethereal, alien, and beautiful.

**Variations:**
- Horizontal drift through a bioluminescent bloom — walls of glowing plankton
- Anglerfish lure close-up in total darkness, only the lure visible
- Deep-sea thermal vent: glowing bacteria, tube worms, mineral chimneys

### Great Barrier Reef Coral Spawning
**Prompt:** Time-lapse underwater shot of coral spawning on the Great Barrier Reef. Bundles of coral eggs and sperm rise from the reef like underwater snow, filling the water column. Moonlight filters down from the surface. The camera slowly tilts up to follow the rising spawn. Schools of plankton-feeding fish dart through the clouds of eggs. Wide angle 14mm, natural moonlight, no strobes. The reef itself is a tapestry of color — brain coral, staghorn, plate coral.

**Settings:** 16:9, 20 seconds

**Expected result:** Rare natural phenomenon captured as underwater "snowfall." Ethereal moonlight, rising particles, living reef texture.

**Variations:**
- Daylight version: vibrant reef colors, fish activity, no spawning
- Night predator version: reef sharks hunting through the spawn clouds
- Macro version: single coral polyp releasing its bundle in slow motion

### Frozen Lake Bubbles
**Prompt:** Top-down macro shot of methane bubbles trapped in Abraham Lake's crystal-clear ice. Each frozen bubble stack is a unique abstract sculpture — white, silver, pale blue. Camera moves slowly across the ice surface, alternating between sharp focus on bubbles and soft focus on ice texture patterns below. Subtle reflections of clouds on the ice surface. The color palette is entirely cool: arctic blue, white, silver, with occasional deep blue where ice is thickest. Shot on macro lens, f/5.6, natural overcast light.

**Settings:** 16:9, 15 seconds

**Expected result:** Abstract natural art from frozen methane bubbles. Clean, minimal, and mesmerizing. Could be mistaken for a CGI render.

**Variations:**
- Add a person in red jacket standing on the ice for scale (wide shot)
- Cracking ice version: hairline cracks spreading through the bubble formations
- Sunrise version: warm orange light catching the ice crystals

## Tips: How to Write Cinematic Sora Prompts

### Camera Movements
- **Dolly in/out**: "Camera dollies slowly toward the subject" (physical camera movement)
- **Push in**: "Camera pushes in" (similar to dolly but can mean digital zoom)
- **Tracking**: "Camera tracks alongside the subject at walking pace"
- **Crane/Jib**: "Camera cranes up from eye level to 30ft overhead"
- **Drone/Aerial**: "Aerial shot descending from 200ft to 50ft"
- **Orbit**: "Camera orbits 360 degrees around the subject"
- **Steadicam**: "Steadicam follows behind the subject, smooth and low"
- **Handheld**: "Slight handheld shake, documentary feel"

### Lighting Conditions
- **Golden hour**: "Warm golden light, long shadows, 30 minutes before sunset"
- **Blue hour**: "Deep blue ambient light, 20 minutes after sunset"
- **Volumetric**: "Volumetric light shafts through fog/dust"
- **Rim light**: "Strong backlight creating rim light on subject edges"
- **Chiaroscuro**: "Dramatic light-dark contrast, single hard light source"
- **Overcast**: "Soft, diffused overcast light, no hard shadows"
- **Neon**: "Neon lighting — pink, blue, electric green reflecting off wet surfaces"

### Film Stocks & Looks
- **Kodak Vision3 500T**: "Tungsten film stock, warm grain, cinematic color science"
- **Kodak Ektachrome**: "Saturated slide film look, vivid colors, fine grain"
- **Fujifilm Pro 400H**: "Pastel tones, soft highlight roll-off, gentle grain"
- **35mm Film**: "35mm film grain, natural color, slight halation on highlights"
- **IMAX**: "IMAX-quality clarity, enormous scale, clean but not sterile"

### Pro Tips
1. **Specify the lens**: "21mm wide angle" or "200mm telephoto" — this dramatically changes the look
2. **Name a cinematographer**: "In the style of Roger Deakins" or "Emmanuel Lubezki lighting"
3. **Describe the mood, not just the scene**: "A sense of quiet loneliness" vs "empty room"
4. **Give camera speed**: "Camera moves at walking pace" or "400x time-lapse speed"
5. **Include negative cues**: "No camera shake, no lens flare, no artificial lighting"
