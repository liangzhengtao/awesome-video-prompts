# Photorealistic Scene Prompts for Runway Gen-3

> Tool: Runway Gen-3 | Style: Photorealistic | Last updated: 2026

## Portraits & People

### The Baker at Dawn
**Prompt:** A middle-aged baker with flour-dusted forearms shapes sourdough loaves at 4 AM. Single warm overhead bulb lights the scene — deep shadows, golden highlights. Their hands work the dough with practiced precision — fold, push, turn, fold. Flour particles float in the light beam. The wooden workbench is scarred from decades of use. Steam rises from the oven behind them. Camera: medium close-up, handheld with slight movement, 50mm lens. Film grain, warm color grade, Kodak Vision3 500T look.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Intimate portrait of craftsperson at work. Warm, textured, lived-in. The flour particles in the light are the hero detail.

**Variations:**
- Pastry chef: precise chocolate tempering, cooler lighting, more clinical
- Street food vendor: wok fire, night market, chaotic energy
- Butcher: cold room, hanging carcasses, utilitarian lighting

### Commuter in the Rain
**Prompt:** A woman in a dark wool coat stands at a bus stop in the rain. She holds no umbrella — her hair is damp, drops on her glasses. She stares at something off-screen, expression unreadable. Headlights of passing cars sweep across her face in rhythm. The bus shelter light flickers. Her breath is visible in the cold. Behind her, the city is a bokeh wall of neon and streetlights. She blinks slowly. Camera: static, eye level, 85mm portrait lens, shallow depth of f/1.4. Color: desaturated with isolated warm tones on her face only.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Cinematic street portrait with atmospheric depth. The rain, bokeh, and warm light on cold skin create a mood piece. Could be a film still.

**Variations:**
- Man with headphones: same setup, different emotional read — he's smiling slightly
- Child at the same bus stop: different scale, different vulnerability
- Summer version: same spot, heat haze, sweat, harsh midday light

### Old Man and His Garden
**Prompt:** Close-up of weathered hands planting a seedling in dark, rich soil. Nails are dirty, knuckles scarred. Camera follows the hands — they pat the soil gently, almost lovingly. Pull back to reveal: an elderly man kneeling in a backyard garden. Tomato plants, sunflowers, herbs in terracotta pots. Morning dew on everything. A cat watches from a porch railing. The man stands up slowly — knees protesting. He surveys his garden, hands on hips, satisfied. A single sunflower turns toward the camera as if watching him. Natural lighting, golden hour, shot on 35mm with vintage lens — slight vignetting and warm halation.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Quiet dignity of daily ritual. The hands tell a lifetime story. The sunflower turning is the subtle magical-realist touch. Warm, golden, Terrence Malick vibes.

**Variations:**
- Grandmother with herbs: different cultural context (Chinese or Italian garden)
- Community garden: multiple people, diverse, shared space
- Winter: dormant garden, same man planning next season with seed catalogs

## Wildlife

### Hummingbird in Flight
**Prompt:** Ultra slow-motion (1000fps) of a rufous hummingbird hovering at a trumpet flower. Wings are a blur of motion — individual wingbeats become visible. The bird's gorget (throat patch) shifts from copper to blazing orange as it turns its head. Its long beak probes the flower. Pollen dust puffs out. The bird's eye — round, dark, intelligent — looks directly at camera for one second. Then it zips away at impossible speed, leaving a single feather drifting in the air. Macro lens, natural sunlight, shallow depth of field. The background is a green bokeh garden.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Extreme slow-motion reveals the hummingbird's impossible physics. The direct eye contact creates connection. The departure is sudden and final.

**Variations:**
- Bee-eater catching a bee: mid-air acrobatics, insect in beak
- Kingfisher diving: underwater split-shot, entry splash
- Owl in flight: silent, powerful, feathers fanning in slow-motion

### Wolves in Snow
**Prompt:** A pack of five gray wolves moving through deep snow in a boreal forest. The alpha leads, breaking trail. Others follow in single file. Snow falls heavily — large flakes catching in fur. The alpha stops, raises its head, and howls. Breath billows in the cold air. The other wolves stop. The camera is at wolf eye-level, tracking alongside them. Pine trees heavy with snow frame the scene. Light: flat overcast, cold blue-gray, no shadows. Sound design: only wind, snow crunching, and the howl echoing. Shot on telephoto (200mm), compressed perspective.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Wild, cold, pack dynamics. The howl is the emotional peak. The compressed telephoto makes the wolves feel close and powerful. "Frozen Planet" quality.

**Variations:**
- Arctic fox in blizzard: small, white, nearly invisible
- Moose in morning mist: enormous, solitary, moody
- Salmon run: bears fishing in rapids, splash, action

### Coral Reef Micro-World
**Prompt:** Macro underwater shot of a single coral head — a city in miniature. A cleaner wrasse picks parasites from a moray eel's mouth (symbiotic relationship). A clownfish darts in and out of its anemone home. A mantis shrimp — iridescent, alien — lurks in a burrow. Pistol shrimp snap, creating tiny shockwave bubbles. Camera slowly orbits the coral head. Lighting: dappled sunlight from above, natural blue-green water tone. Individual polyps visible on the coral surface, some extended to feed. Shot on macro underwater rig, natural light only.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** A complete ecosystem on one coral head. Multiple species interacting naturally. Macro detail reveals a world invisible to the casual snorkeler. BBC "Blue Planet" quality.

**Variations:**
- Night reef: different inhabitants, bioluminescence, predators hunting
- Mangrove roots: above and below waterline, juvenile fish nursery
- Deep reef: artificial light only, unusual species, alien atmosphere

## Urban Environments

### Tokyo Morning Commute
**Prompt:** Shinjuku Station platform at 8:15 AM. A train arrives — doors open with a chime. The crowd moves as one organism: people exiting, people entering, a seamless choreography of efficiency. The camera is on the platform, shooting through the crowd at waist level. Legs and feet create a forest of movement. Suits, briefcases, school bags. The doors close. The train departs. The platform empties in 30 seconds. A single person remains, looking at their phone, unaware the train has gone. They look up. The platform is empty. They blink. Shot on Sony Venice 2, 28mm, natural station lighting, documentary style.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** The hypnotic rhythm of Japanese commuting. The crowd-as-organism is the spectacle. The lone person left behind is the human comedy beat.

**Variations:**
- NYC subway: grittier, more chaotic, different energy
- London Tube: queue discipline, rain-soaked umbrellas
- Mumbai local: extreme density, hanging from doors, different physics

### Rainy Night Crosswalk
**Prompt:** Overhead (bird's eye) shot of a rainy city crosswalk at night. Pedestrians with umbrellas cross in organized chaos — their umbrellas are colored dots: black, red, clear, blue, yellow. Wet asphalt reflects every light source — traffic signals, neon signs, headlights. The crosswalk stripes are bright white against black wet road. Cars wait, wipers going. One person crosses without an umbrella, head down, hands in pockets. They're the only one without color above them. Camera holds static overhead. The scene is a living Mondrian painting.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Abstract-from-above urban pattern. The colored umbrellas become paint dots on a wet canvas. The umbrella-less person is the focal point — exposed, human.

**Variations:**
- Snowy version: footprints in fresh snow, different textures
- Tokyo scramble crossing: massive crowd, organized chaos from above
- Empty intersection at 3 AM: single car passing through red light

### Construction Dawn
**Prompt:** Time-lapse (1000x) of a construction site from a fixed camera position. Steel beams rise floor by floor. Cranes swing materials into position. Workers are motion-blurred streaks. Clouds race overhead. The building grows from foundation to 20 stories. The sun moves across the sky — golden hour to golden hour, 365 days compressed to 8 seconds. The surrounding city changes subtly: new lights appear, trees grow, seasons cycle (snow, rain, sun, autumn). The final frame: a completed glass tower reflecting the sunset. The camera is exactly where it started — but the view has changed forever.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** "How it's made" meets time-lapse photography. The construction is the main event; the seasonal changes are the bonus layer. Satisfying progress visualization.

**Variations:**
- House construction: wooden frame, more personal, a home being born
- Bridge: spanning a river, different engineering challenge
- Demolition: the reverse — a building coming down, rubble and dust

## Rural & Natural

### Foggy Vineyard Morning
**Prompt:** Dawn in a Tuscan vineyard. Dense fog sits in the valley — only the tops of cypress trees and the bell tower of a distant hilltop church emerge above the fog line. The camera is at ground level, looking down a row of grapevines. The vines are heavy with purple grapes, dew-covered. A tractor's headlights cut through the fog in the distance, moving slowly. As the sun rises, the fog begins to burn off — it peels back like a curtain, revealing more of the landscape in layers: first the vines, then the rolling hills, then the full valley. Shot on ARRI Alexa, 35mm, natural light, subtle film grain.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Atmospheric landscape transformation as fog lifts. The layered reveal is the structure. Warm Italian countryside, painterly light.

**Variations:**
- Japanese rice terraces: similar fog, different crop, pagoda in background
- Lavender fields: purple rows, bees visible, Provençal light
- English countryside: green rolling hills, stone walls, sheep

### Wild Horses Running
**Prompt:** A herd of wild mustangs gallops across a dusty Nevada plain. Camera at ground level, tracking alongside at full speed — the camera shakes with the pounding hooves. Dust clouds billow behind the herd. A young foal keeps pace with its mother, legs too long, running awkward but determined. The lead stallion turns, checking the herd. Manes and tails fly. Shot on long lens (300mm) from a vehicle parallel to the herd. The light is late afternoon — long shadows, warm dust catching golden sun. The horses crest a ridge and silhouetted against the sky for a brief moment before disappearing over the other side.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Raw, kinetic energy of wild horses at full gallop. The camera matching their speed creates visceral immersion. The silhouette on the ridge is the hero frame.

**Variations:**
- Camels in desert: slower, different gait, sand instead of dust
- Elephants crossing a river: spray, trumpeting, slow powerful movement
- Birds in murmuration: aerial version of coordinated group movement

### Frozen Waterfall
**Prompt:** A 100-foot waterfall in mid-winter. The water still flows, but massive ice columns have formed around the falling water — a frozen frame of the cascade's shape. Water flows through the center of the ice, visible through the translucent blue-green formations. Icicles hang from overhanging rocks like a cathedral organ. Camera cranes up from the frozen pool at the base, following the ice columns to the top where water still plunges over the cliff edge. Mist rises where water meets ice, creating a cloud that catches sunlight — a tiny rainbow. The color palette is entirely cool: arctic blue, ice white, shadow blue-violet. The only warm element: the rainbow.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Nature's ice sculpture — a waterfall half-frozen in time. The crane shot reveals the full scale. The rainbow in the mist adds a moment of warmth to the frozen scene.

**Variations:**
- Iceland: geothermal hot springs meeting ice — steam and frost
- Niagara Falls: massive scale, ice bridge, tourist viewing platform
- Japanese winter waterfall: snow-covered rocks, simpler composition

## Extreme Weather

### Lightning Over the Plains
**Prompt:** Time-lapse of a supercell thunderstorm over the Great Plains at night. The mesocyclone rotates ominously — a massive wall cloud lit from within by continuous lightning. Multiple cloud-to-ground bolts strike simultaneously, illuminating the flat landscape in stroboscopic flashes. An anvil cloud spreads overhead, blocking stars. Rain curtains are visible as dark sheets beneath the cloud base. The camera is fixed on a tripod, 24mm wide angle, long exposure per frame. The storm is the size of a state — the camera captures only a fraction. Bolt after bolt after bolt.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Apocalyptic storm system at full force. The continuous lightning creates a stroboscopic effect. The scale is humbling. National Geographic storm chasing quality.

**Variations:**
- Tornado forming: funnel cloud descending, debris visible
- Dust storm haboob: wall of sand approaching a city
- Hurricane eye wall: viewed from satellite perspective, organized rotation

### Avalanche Release
**Prompt:** A cornice of snow on a mountain ridge cracks and releases. The avalanche begins as a slab — coherent, massive — then breaks into powder as it accelerates down the slope. Camera is on the opposite ridge, telephoto, tracking the slide. The powder cloud expands, overtaking the sliding debris. Trees at the base of the slope are engulfed. The powder cloud rises hundreds of feet, mushrooming. The sound design: the crack, then the roar, then the silence. After the cloud settles: the slope is unrecognizable — smooth, white, transformed. A single tree sticks out of the debris field.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** Nature's raw destructive power. The scale shift from snow slab to powder cloud to transformed landscape is the narrative. The lone tree is the survivor.

**Volcanic Lightning:** The ash plume generates its own lightning — bolts within the eruption cloud
**Ice calving:** A glacier face collapses into the ocean, creating waves
**Sandstorm approaching:** Wall of orange sand consuming a desert road

### Tornado in Wheat Field
**Prompt:** A fully-formed tornado touches down in a golden Kansas wheat field. The funnel is half a mile wide — brown at the base (debris), gray in the middle, white at the top where it connects to the storm cloud. Wheat bends in concentric circles around the funnel. The camera is close — too close — on a wide lens. The tornado fills the frame. Debris flies: wheat stalks, dirt, a piece of fencing. The sky is green-black (the color that means hail). The camera shakes from the wind. The tornado moves left to right across the frame, consuming everything in its path. It sounds like a freight train inside a hurricane.

**Settings:** 16:9, 8 seconds

**Expected result:** In-your-face tornado footage. Too close for comfort. The green sky and wheat contrast with the gray funnel. The camera shake adds authenticity.

**Variations:**
- Waterspout: over ocean, cleaner funnel, water spray at base
- Multiple vortex: several small funnels orbiting the main tornado
- Aftermath: calm, blue sky, destruction path visible from above

## Tips: Achieving Photorealism with Runway

### Avoiding the Uncanny Valley
- **Don't request close-up faces talking**: Runway struggles with lip sync and micro-expressions
- **Focus on environments over faces**: Landscapes, interiors, and wide shots are more reliable
- **Add imperfections**: "Slight film grain," "dust on lens," "lens distortion" — perfection looks fake
- **Include motion blur**: "Slight motion blur on fast movements" — too-sharp digital footage looks wrong

### Realism Boosters
- **Name the camera and lens**: "Shot on ARRI Alexa, 50mm Master Prime" grounds the look
- **Specify lighting conditions**: "Natural overcast daylight" vs "single tungsten practical"
- **Add texture cues**: "Brick wall with visible mortar," "skin with visible pores"
- **Include environmental interaction**: "Steam rising," "dust particles in light," "condensation on glass"

### What Runway Does Best for Realism
- **Landscapes and nature**: Excellent at skies, water, terrain
- **Weather effects**: Rain, fog, snow, clouds — strong
- **Animal motion**: Good at wildlife, especially common animals
- **Architecture**: Buildings, interiors, urban scenes — very capable
- **Vehicles in motion**: Cars, trains, planes — solid
