## Impressionist & Fine Art

### Monet's Water Lilies in Motion
**Prompt:** A pond of water lilies painted in the style of Claude Monet's "Water Lilies" series. The scene is alive — lily pads drift slowly on still water, reflections of clouds move across the surface. The painting style is loose brushstrokes, visible canvas texture, dappled color — blues, greens, pinks, whites melting into each other. A dragonfly lands on a lily pad, its wings catching light (the only sharp element in the scene). The impressionist style means nothing has hard edges — everything is suggestion, light, and color. Camera slowly pans across the pond surface.

**Settings:** 16:9, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** A living Monet painting. Every element has visible brushstroke texture. The dragonfly is the only "realistic" detail, making the painterly world feel inhabited. Dreamy, luminous, peaceful.

**Variations:**
- Van Gogh's "Starry Night": swirling sky, thick impasto texture, stars pulsing
- Klimt's "The Kiss": gold leaf patterns, decorative mosaic, figures embracing
- Hokusai's "Great Wave": woodblock print style, wave crashing, foam particles

### Starry Night Village
**Prompt:** A small European village at the base of rolling hills, painted in the style of Vincent van Gogh's "The Starry Night." The sky dominates — swirling spirals of deep blue, cobalt, and ultramarine. Stars pulse with halos of yellow-white light. The crescent moon radiates concentric rings. Below: cottages with warm glowing windows, a church steeple pointing into the sky. Cypress trees flame upward like dark green fire. Everything has thick impasto brushstrokes — visible, textured, alive. The village lights flicker. Wind moves through the cypress trees. The sky itself rotates slowly — the swirls are in constant motion. The painting breathes.

**Settings:** 16:9, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** Van Gogh's style animated — the swirls move, the stars pulse, the village lives. The thick texture is visible and consistent throughout. Turbulent sky above peaceful village: the van Gogh tension.

**Variations:**
- "Café Terrace at Night": warm yellows, outdoor seating, cobblestones
- "Wheatfield with Crows": ominous sky, golden wheat, three paths diverging
- Japanese cherry blossom: van Gogh's style applied to sakura, not European landscape

### Vermeer Interior
**Prompt:** A woman stands by a window in a 17th-century Dutch interior, painted in the style of Johannes Vermeer. Soft north light enters from the left, illuminating her face and the wall behind her in a perfect gradient. She reads a letter, her expression gentle and private. The room is sparse: a table with a blue cloth, a map on the far wall, a tiled floor in black and white. Every surface has Vermeer's signature soft focus and luminous quality. Camera: static, 50mm perspective, as if we're looking through a Dutch doorway. The light moves slowly — a cloud passing outside shifts the illumination for a moment, then returns.

**Settings:** 16:9, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** A living Vermeer painting. The light is the subject — how it falls on skin, fabric, and walls. The quiet domestic scene has the weight of a private moment we're witnessing. Intimate, luminous, still.

**Variations:**
- "Girl with a Pearl Earring": direct gaze, dark background, the earring glowing
- "The Milkmaid": pouring milk in a continuous stream, kitchen still life
- "The Art of Painting": painter working on a canvas, meta-recursive scene

## Cyberpunk & Sci-Fi Aesthetics

### Blade Runner Street Market
**Prompt:** A street food market in a cyberpunk Asian megacity. Rain falls constantly. Neon signs in Japanese, Chinese, and Korean glow above each stall — pink, blue, electric cyan. Steam rises from noodle bowls and wok fires. Holographic advertisements float between buildings: animated product logos, floating koi fish, a giant face selling something. People in the crowd wear a mix of high-tech and low-tech: augmented reality visors, traditional rain hats, LED-embedded jackets. The camera pushes through the crowd at street level. Puddles reflect everything. The atmosphere is dense — rain, steam, neon, holograms all layered together. Ridley Scott meets Katsuhiro Otomo.

**Settings:** 2.39:1, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** Cyberpunk sensory overload. Every surface is wet, every light source is neon or holographic. The crowd creates human texture. Dense, beautiful, dystopian. "Blade Runner" meets "Akira."

**Variations:**
- Corporate tower interior: sterile white, holographic assistants, glass and chrome
- Undercity: dark, dripping, mechanical, industrial decay
- Sky taxi: aerial view of the neon grid from above the rain

### Neon Samurai
**Prompt:** A lone samurai stands in a rain-soaked alley. Traditional armor — but augmented: LED strips along the chest plate glow blue-white. A katana blade hums with electromagnetic energy. The samurai's helmet visor displays a HUD (visible as faint holographic text). Rain runs off the armor in rivulets. Behind the samurai: a wall of neon kanji (侍, 剣, 道). The samurai draws the blade in a single fluid motion — the camera follows the blade tip in slow motion. The blade catches every neon light in the alley, creating a streak of prismatic color. The samurai holds the blade at guard. Lightning flashes — for one frame, we see the alley is full of defeated opponents.

**Settings:** 9:16 (vertical), 10 seconds

**Expected result:** Traditional meets futuristic. The samurai is ancient and advanced simultaneously. The neon reflections on the blade are the hero visual moment. The lightning flash reveal is the narrative hook.

**Variations:**
- Ninja: all-black, stealth tech, disappearing into digital static
- Ronin: weathered, damaged armor, wandering through wasteland
- Dual: two augmented samurai facing off, mirrored neon

### Holographic City Planning
**Prompt:** A transparent glass desk in a minimalist white room. On the desk: a holographic city model, 2 feet tall, floating. Buildings, roads, parks, all rendered in translucent blue light. A hand reaches in and moves a building — the city rearranges itself in response, traffic patterns flowing around the new layout. The hand rotates the model, and we see the city from below — infrastructure, subway lines, water pipes, all visible as different colored light layers. The city pulses: data flowing through it like blood through veins. Day-night cycle plays in fast-forward inside the hologram — tiny lights going on and off. The hand swipes, and the hologram zooms into a single street — we can see individual people as points of light.

**Settings:** 16:9, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** Sci-fi city planning interface. The holographic model is detailed and responsive. The hand interaction makes it feel like a real tool. Clean, futuristic, data-rich.

**Variations:**
- Medical: holographic human body, organs highlighted, disease markers in red
- Stellar map: holographic galaxy, planets orbiting, ship routes plotted
- DNA: holographic double helix, base pairs editable, genetic engineering interface

## Film Noir

### The Detective's Office
**Prompt:** A private detective's office, 1940s. Venetian blinds cast striped shadows across everything. A single desk lamp creates a pool of light on a cluttered desk — case files, a whiskey glass, a rotary phone, an ashtray with a still-smoking cigarette. The detective (seen only from the chest down — trench coat, fedora) sits behind the desk. Smoke curls upward through the lamplight. Rain hits the window behind the blinds. A shadow appears on the frosted glass door: someone is standing outside. The door opens. A silhouette enters — heels click on hardwood. The detective's hand moves to the desk drawer. Camera: static, low angle, looking up at the detective. High contrast black and white, deep shadows, a single hard light source.

**Settings:** 4:3 (classic ratio), 10 seconds

**Expected result:** Textbook film noir: striped light, smoke, rain, shadows, mystery. The 4:3 aspect ratio and black-and-white sell the period. We never see the detective's face — that's the point.

**Variations:**
- Alley chase: fedora and trench coat running through rain-lit alleys
- Femme fatale: close-up of red lips, cigarette holder, veiled eyes
- Rooftop confrontation: city lights below, two figures, one gun

### Femme Fatale Entrance
**Prompt:** A dark jazz club entrance. A woman in a floor-length black dress steps through the doorway. Camera at low angle, looking up. She's backlit — only her silhouette and the smoke from her cigarette holder are visible. She steps forward into a shaft of light from a hanging pendant lamp. Her face is revealed gradually: high cheekbones, dark eyes, red lips. She scans the room. Every head turns. She walks toward the camera — the camera backs up, keeping her in medium shot. The club behind her: dim amber light, saxophone player, haze of smoke. She stops. Looks directly into camera. A smile — but it doesn't reach her eyes. She takes a drag. The cigarette ember glows, illuminating just her eyes.

**Settings:** 2.39:1 (anamorphic), 10 seconds

**Expected result:** The iconic noir entrance. Every element — lighting, movement, gaze — builds the archetype. The final cigarette glow on her eyes is the signature shot. Dangerous, beautiful, unknowable.

**Variations:**
- Male version: a figure in a dark suit, hat pulled low, same entrance
- Modern noir: same staging, contemporary setting, smartphone instead of cigarette
- Color noir: desaturated color with isolated red (her lips, the cigarette ember)

### Rain on the Window
**Prompt:** Close-up of rain streaming down a window at night. Through the wet glass, a neon sign across the street glows — "HOTEL" in red. The raindrops distort the neon, each drop acting as a lens creating tiny inverted images of the sign. Camera slowly pushes in, going from wide to macro. The individual raindrops become the landscape — rivulets merging, drops racing each other, the neon reflections multiplying infinitely in the water. The focus shifts: pull focus from the rain on the glass to the hotel sign, then back. Each shift changes the mood: blurry rain = abstract beauty, sharp sign = noir narrative. Someone's hand enters frame and touches the glass from the inside.

**Settings:** 16:9, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** Rain-on-glass as a cinematic language. The focus pulls shift between abstract and narrative. The hand touch is the human moment in an otherwise atmospheric-only shot. Moody, melancholic, beautiful.

**Variations:**
- Snow instead of rain: slower, softer, different light scatter
- Car window: driving through city rain, streetlights streaking
- Bathroom mirror: steam and water droplets, someone's face half-visible

## Studio Ghibli & Anime

### Ghibli Countryside
**Prompt:** A rolling green hillside under a vast sky with enormous cumulus clouds. A dirt path winds through wildflowers — daisies, poppies, cornflowers. A young girl in a blue dress runs along the path, her red hat blown off by the wind. She chases it. The wind carries the hat past a field of sunflowers taller than her. In the distance: a small village with red-tile rooftops and a church steeple. A steam train chugs along tracks at the hill's base. The art style is Hayao Miyazaki's watercolor-meets-animation: soft edges, saturated but natural colors, detailed backgrounds, slightly simplified characters. The light is perpetual golden hour — warm, long shadows, gentle.

**Settings:** 16:9, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** A living Ghibli landscape. Lush, detailed backgrounds that feel like places you could walk into. The girl's movement is expressive but not hyper-realistic. The sky and clouds are the real star — Miyazaki always gave 70% of the frame to the sky.

**Variations:**
- Forest spirit: a Totoro-like creature emerges from ancient trees
- Floating castle: Laputa-style architecture hovering above clouds
- Bathhouse: Spirited Away-style Japanese bathhouse at night, spirits arriving

### Makoto Shinkai Rain
**Prompt:** A cityscape in the style of Makoto Shinkai — every surface is hyper-detailed and luminous. Rain falls in Shinkai's signature style: each raindrop is individually rendered, catching light like tiny prisms. A teenage boy stands at a train crossing, waiting. The train passes — a blur of reflected sky and rain. On the other side of the tracks, a girl with a translucent umbrella waits. Their eyes meet through the gap between train cars — a split-second connection. The train passes. She's gone. He looks at the sky: the clouds are breaking, and Shinkai's signature "god ray" sunbeams pour through the gap, illuminating the wet tracks. Everything is hyper-saturated: the sky is impossibly blue, the wet surfaces mirror everything.

**Settings:** 16:9, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** Makoto Shinkai's hyperreal anime style: more beautiful than reality, every surface reflective, every light source dramatic. The train-crossing near-miss is his signature narrative. The god rays after rain are his visual signature.

**Variations:**
- Cherry blossom: petals falling in extreme slow-motion, each one detailed
- Night sky: Shinkai's star fields, Milky Way visible, two figures on a rooftop
- Sunset: impossibly colorful sky, silhouettes on a hilltop, distance between two people

### Akira Slide Recreation
**Prompt:** A motorcycle rider in a red leather jacket slides sideways through a rain-soaked Neo-Tokyo street. The motorcycle's front wheel is locked, rear wheel spinning, creating a spray of sparks and water. The rider's body is leaned at an extreme angle — knee nearly touching the ground. The camera is low, tracking the slide at motorcycle speed. The cityscape behind: massive concrete buildings, elevated highways, protest banners. The art style is Katsuhiro Otomo's "Akira": ultra-detailed mechanical design, realistic anatomy, dense urban environment, dramatic perspective. The slide seems to last forever — frozen in the moment between control and crash.

**Settings:** 2.39:1, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** The most iconic anime motorcycle moment recreated. Otomo's detail level: every rivet on the bike, every raindrop, every crack in the asphalt. Kinetic energy frozen in art.

**Variations:**
- Kaneda's bike: the specific red motorcycle from Akira, same slide
- Ghost in the Shell: thermoptic camo flickering, different cyberpunk aesthetic
- Cowboy Bebop: jazz-infused, Spike's fighter jet in a dogfight

## Wes Anderson

### Symmetrical Hotel Lobby
**Prompt:** A perfectly symmetrical hotel lobby in Wes Anderson's signature style. Center frame: a concierge desk with a brass bell. Behind it: a wall of tiny wooden pigeonhole mail slots (144 of them, arranged in a perfect grid). To the left: an elevator with ornate brass doors. To the right: a matching elevator. A bellhop in a burgundy uniform with gold buttons stands perfectly centered. The color palette is pastel: dusty pink walls, sage green carpet, cream molding. Everything is centered, balanced, geometrically perfect. The bellhop dings the bell once. A guest (in a matching but different shade of burgundy) enters from the left elevator. They cross to the right elevator. Symmetry maintained. The camera: dead center, symmetrical, tracking shot.

**Settings:** 16:9, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** Pure Wes Anderson: bilateral symmetry, pastel palette, deadpan movement, every detail curated. The symmetry is the joke and the beauty simultaneously. "The Grand Budapest Hotel" energy.

**Variations:**
- Train compartment: symmetrical seats, matching passengers, window view
- Library: symmetrical shelves, ladder in center, librarian on each side
- Kitchen: symmetrical counters, two chefs doing mirror-image prep work

### Cross-Section Montage
**Prompt:** A building cross-section in the style of Wes Anderson's "The French Dispatch" — we see all floors simultaneously, dollhouse-style. Top floor: a writer at a typewriter, papers flying. Middle floor: a dinner party in progress, guests frozen mid-conversation. Bottom floor: a laundry room, a cat sleeping on warm towels. Each floor is color-coded: top = blue, middle = red, bottom = yellow. Characters on each floor are unaware of the others. A single thread connects them: a leak from the top floor drips through to the bottom. The camera dollies across each floor at the same height — as if each floor is a stage set. The framing is flat, frontal, deliberately artificial.

**Settings:** 16:9, 10 seconds

**Expected result:** The Wes Anderson dollhouse aesthetic — simultaneous storytelling across connected spaces. Color-coded floors, flat staging, the water-leak connection. Charming, literary, meticulously composed.

**Variations:**
- Cross-section of a ship: different cabin classes, same journey
- Underground station: platforms, tunnels, ticket halls, all visible
- Tree house: rooms in different branches, connected by rope bridges

## Tips: How to Combine Styles and Reference Artists

### Style Transfer Formula
1. **Name the style explicitly**: "In the style of [artist/movement]" — Runway responds well to direct references
2. **Describe the technique**: "Oil on canvas, visible brushstrokes" or "Woodblock print, flat color, no gradients"
3. **Keep the scene simple**: The more complex the scene, the more the style may be diluted
4. **Specify what stays realistic**: "The water is stylized but the fish is anatomically accurate"

### Combining Two Styles
- Use "meets" format: "Monet's impressionism meets cyberpunk architecture"
- Use "in the style of X, with elements of Y": "Van Gogh's sky, Hokusai's waves"
- Be specific about which elements get which style: "Art Nouveau borders, ukiyo-e figures"

### Common Style Keywords
- **Painting**: oil painting, watercolor, gouache, acrylic, fresco, tempera
- **Drawing**: charcoal, pencil sketch, ink wash, pen and ink, pastel
- **Print**: woodblock, lithograph, screenprint, etching, linocut
- **Digital**: vector art, pixel art, 3D render, cel-shaded, low-poly
- **Film**: black and white, Technicolor, cross-processed, bleach bypass

### Pitfalls
- Too many style references: pick 1-2, not 5
- Conflicting styles: "photorealistic anime" is contradictory — choose one
- Missing subject: style without scene description gives random results
- Over-detailing: leave room for the AI to interpret the style
