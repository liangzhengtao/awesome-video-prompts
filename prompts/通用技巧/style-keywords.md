## How to Use This Reference

### The 2-4 Rule
Pick **2-4 style keywords** per prompt. More than that creates conflict.

**Good:** `cinematic, warm, shallow depth of field`
**Bad:** `cinematic, anime, minimalist, chaotic, noir, vintage, retro, futuristic`

### Combining Categories
Mix one keyword from each category for a complete style direction:

```
[Visual Style] + [Color] + [Texture] + [Mood] + [Lighting]
```

**Example:**
`cinematic` + `warm amber tones` + `film grain` + `nostalgic` + `golden hour`
= "A cinematic scene with warm amber tones, film grain texture, nostalgic mood, in golden hour light."

### Tool-Specific Notes

**Sora:** Responds well to film references and cinematographer names. Style keywords + camera/lens references work best.

**Runway:** Strong with artistic style keywords. `In the style of [artist]` is very effective. Less responsive to camera/lens specifications.

**Pika:** Keep style keywords to 1-2 max. Simpler keywords work better than complex combinations. `Anime style`, `3D render`, `cinematic` are reliable.

**Kling:** Handles realistic and cultural styles well. Chinese aesthetic keywords (水墨, 古风, 工笔) are strong. Human motion descriptions matter more than style keywords.
